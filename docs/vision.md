# Career Companion - Product Vision

## 1. What problem does Career Companion solve?
- **Personalized Job Matching:** Helps you find the right job based on your unique skills, interests, and personality rather than just generic searches.
- **Application Tracking:** Keeps track of your job applications and interview pipeline.
- **Skill Development & Upskilling:** Identifies skill gaps and suggests relevant resources to help you upskill.

## 2. Who is the target user?
- Students seeking internships.
- Fresh graduates entering the job market.
- Working professionals looking for a career change.

## 3. What are the MVP features?
- **AI-Powered Application Automation:** Streamlines the application process.
- **Tailored Resume & Cover Letter Generation:** Automatically creates customized resumes and cover letters for specific job descriptions.
- **Centralized Application Tracker:** Storing all application details in a database (e.g., custom resume used, job description, interview notes, and application status).
- **Dynamic Profile Integration:** Automatically updates your professional profile by pulling data from GitHub, personal portfolios, and LinkedIn.
- **Core MVP Checklist:**
  - [ ] Resume parser
  - [ ] Job search
  - [ ] Resume tailoring
  - [ ] Cover letter generation
  - [ ] Job match scoring
  - [ ] Application tracker
  - [ ] Skill gap analysis

## 4. What features are NOT in v1?
- **LinkedIn Integrations:** Auto-posting or updating projects and experiences on LinkedIn.
- **Platform Scope:** No mobile app (Web-only for v1).
- **Advanced Tools:**
  - No interview simulator.
  - No recruiter CRM.
  - No browser extension.
  - No salary prediction.
- **System Constraints:**
  - No cloud sync.
  - No multi-user support.

## 5. Why would someone use this instead of ChatGPT?
- While ChatGPT answers questions like a general consultant, Career Companion acts as an integrated system that manages the full workflow, connects to external professional profiles, persists application data, and delivers tailor-made documents directly matching specific target jobs.
